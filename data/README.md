# Short-term wealth monitoring data

The legacy root `products_data.json` is preserved unchanged for backward compatibility.

`products.json` is generated by the scheduled workflow. All legacy records are **unverified** and must **not** be presented as confirmed currently-on-sale bank wealth products. `sale_status: unknown` means no verified sale information. Missing values stay null.

Scope: cash management, daily-open, 1–30-day minimum holding, and 1–30-day fixed-term products, with redemption restrictions separately disclosed. Funds and bank wealth management products must not be mixed in bank-only rankings.

Official disclosure sources and bank public feeds are not yet connected. No remaining quota or real-time purchase availability is asserted. The workflow only normalizes the current seed until authorized adapters are implemented.
